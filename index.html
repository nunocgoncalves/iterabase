What you are getting

Not a chatbot. A coworker with a role, a process and tools.

AI is the engine, not the product. The work gets done through tools: the systems your team already uses, plus the ones we build where a tool does not exist yet. You approve every tool a digital employee is allowed to touch. The model decides what to reach for, inside those boundaries.

It owns a process

Each digital employee is responsible for one repeatable process, start to finish, the same way you would hand it to a person on your team.

It knows when to ask

One role in detail

A dispatch planner that finishes before the shift starts.

A role we build for a transport operation. It starts on a schedule, runs without anyone in the loop, and hands back only the decisions that need judgment. Every other role follows the same shape.

01

Read the open orders

Every order awaiting dispatch, taken straight from the ERP at a fixed time each morning.

02

Read the fleet

Available trucks, capacities and load specifications as they stand today, not as they were planned last week.

03

Build the routes

Loads assigned against truck, load and destination constraints, national and international, using the rules your planners already apply.

04

Compile the manifests

One manifest per route, in the format your drivers and customers already receive.

05

Write back to the ERP

Routes created in the system of record, not in a side tool nobody maintains.

06

Hand over the exceptions

Anything ambiguous goes to a named person with the context attached, and the work resumes as soon as they answer.

Each step has a defined input, a defined output and a record of what it did. Where the process needs a human signature, that step is built into the process, not bolted on afterwards.

The obvious questions

Asked by everyone who runs a real operation.

"Our processes are too specific to automate."

Your operation as a whole probably is. We do not take the whole operation. We take one process with defined inputs and a defined output, streamline it, and give it to a digital employee. The bespoke judgment stays with your people, as an approval step inside the process.

"Where does our data go?"

Nowhere. The platform and the models both run on your own infrastructure, so your records stay inside your perimeter and under your access controls. No prompt, document or record is sent to an outside provider, and we do not host your operation. Every action the system takes is recorded and auditable.

"What happens when it gets something wrong?"

Most of it never gets that far. Processes carry conditions that stop the work and require a person to approve or decide before it moves on, and anything the digital employee cannot resolve is raised as a blocker that states exactly what it needs. When a result is wrong anyway, your team flags it, the feedback is recorded against the process, and a new attempt runs.

"Nobody here has time to run this."

That is the normal state of an operation at capacity, and it is the reason you are reading this. We need a few hours of your team's time to understand the process and the access to build against it. The building is ours, and one role at a time means nobody has to clear their calendar.
